# Sources and precise scope

1. **D. Karp.** *Positivity of Toeplitz determinants formed by rising factorial series and properties of related polynomials*, [arXiv:1203.1482v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.1482v1). Conjecture 3 on PDF page 2 gives the real-negative-root question. Equation (7) on PDF page 4 identifies the exact order-two transform as `P_n(x)=Q_n^(1,1)(x-1)`. Theorem 1 on the same page establishes degree and coefficient positivity under its stated strict log-concavity hypotheses. Coefficient positivity alone is not a proof of real-rootedness.
2. **Liudmyla Kryvonos.** *Open problems on polynomials, their zero distribution and related questions: 2023*, [arXiv:2312.07754v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.07754v1). Dmitrii Karp's contribution, “Toeplitz determinants of Pochhammers,” original PDF page 3, Conjecture 1, restates the same transform. Page 4 discusses a separate higher-order problem; it is not the theorem addressed here.

The statement in this package is the positive, degree-n, full-support case: `F(t)=c product(1+a_i t)`, `c>0`, every `a_i>0`, `n>=3`. The broader finite-PF wording can involve zero-support conventions; this package does not silently extend to those boundaries. Rising factorials, binomial weights and both shifts are essential.

The manuscript gives the needed coefficient-convolution/Gauss–Lucas argument, beta identity and difference induction in full. Familiar convolution, derivative-root and spacing ideas are not claimed as newly invented general theories. The claim presented is the complete chain for this specific positive order-two transform and its stated residual/linear-weight consequences. No exhaustive literature survey, first-resolution certification or author endorsement is asserted. A later restatement does not by itself establish a conjecture's present status.

Only the source identifiers and mathematical statements checked in the supplied originals are used. The cited articles are not redistributed with this package.
